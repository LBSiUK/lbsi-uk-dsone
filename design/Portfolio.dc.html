Hey, I'm Leon

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Sussex. My work sits at the intersection of software, hardware, and the infrastructure holding everything together.

I've had work experience as a sysadmin at a London-based investment management firm, where I rolled out Windows 11 across the business. I've also worked part-time at a local electronics repair store for several years, and in my spare time I host game servers for friends, mod consoles, code Arduinos, and repair devices for friends and family.

Most skilled in media coding formats, sysadmin, and electronics repair. I code in Python, Java, and PowerShell/Batch. Based in NW London, England.
